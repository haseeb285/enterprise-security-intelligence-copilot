# Information Security Policy
SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION POLICY
NOT AN ACTUAL ORGANIZATION POLICY

## 1. Scope and ownership
The fictional Clockwork Badger Cooperative applies this policy to employees, contractors, and demonstration systems. The Security Steward maintains the policy register and reviews it every twelve months.

## 2. Data classification
Public material may be shared openly. Internal material stays in approved cooperative workspaces. Restricted material, including fictional customer records and access recovery codes, requires named authorization and encryption in transit and at rest. Classification owners reassess labels each quarter.

## 3. Device safeguards
Managed devices must use full-disk encryption, screen lock after ten minutes of inactivity, and security patches within fourteen days of release. Lost devices must be reported to the Security Steward within one hour of discovery.

## 4. Exceptions and review
A documented exception requires a business reason, compensating control, expiry date no later than ninety days, and Security Steward approval. Exception owners review approved exceptions monthly.
