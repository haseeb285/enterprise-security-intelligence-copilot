# Backup and Recovery Policy
SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION POLICY
NOT AN ACTUAL ORGANIZATION POLICY

## 1. Schedule and retention
The fictional cooperative takes encrypted daily backups of critical systems. Daily backups are retained for thirty days and monthly backups for twelve months. Backup access is limited to the recovery operators.

## 2. Isolation and testing
One backup copy must be isolated from routine administrator credentials. Recovery operators test a sample restore every quarter and record integrity checks, elapsed recovery time, and test outcome.

## 3. Recovery targets
The critical ticket service has a recovery time objective of eight hours and a recovery point objective of twenty-four hours. The recovery lead declares a disaster and approves restoration order.

## 4. Disposal
Expired backup media is cryptographically erased and the disposal is recorded. A legal hold issued by the cooperative counsel pauses scheduled destruction for the affected backup set.
