# Incident Response Policy
SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION POLICY
NOT AN ACTUAL ORGANIZATION POLICY

## 1. Reporting and escalation
Anyone suspecting a security incident must notify the Security Steward within thirty minutes of discovery using the incident hotline. Do not wait for certainty. The Steward assigns a case identifier and alerts the on-call responder within fifteen minutes.

## 2. Severity and containment
A confirmed Restricted-data exposure is severity one; the on-call responder begins containment within thirty minutes of classification. Repeated failed logins without confirmed compromise are initially severity three. The responder can isolate a managed device while preserving logs.

## 3. Evidence and communications
Responders record a UTC timeline, evidence source, collector, and cryptographic checksum. The communications lead approves external statements. Only authorized responders access incident evidence; evidence is retained for 180 days after case closure.

## 4. Closure
The incident owner writes a root-cause review within five business days of closure, assigns corrective actions, and verifies completion dates with the Security Steward.
