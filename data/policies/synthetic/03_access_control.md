# Access Control Policy
SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION POLICY
NOT AN ACTUAL ORGANIZATION POLICY

## 1. Joiner and leaver access
Managers request access by role and data classification. The system owner approves access before provisioning. Departing workers lose interactive access within four hours of their recorded departure; shared credentials are then rotated.

## 2. Privileged accounts
Administrators use dedicated privileged accounts separate from daily work identities. Privileged elevation expires after eight hours and requires a ticket identifier, MFA, and approval by a second administrator. Privileged actions are logged for review.

## 3. Quarterly review
System owners review active entitlements every quarter and remove access with no valid work purpose. Access to Restricted data requires explicit owner approval and a six-month recertification.

## 4. Emergency access
Break-glass accounts may be activated only for an outage or urgent incident. The Security Steward reviews their logs within one business day and rotates emergency credentials after use.
