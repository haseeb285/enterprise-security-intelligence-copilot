# Remote Access Policy
SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION POLICY
NOT AN ACTUAL ORGANIZATION POLICY

## 1. Connection requirements
Remote workers connect to Internal or Restricted services through the approved VPN from managed, encrypted devices. VPN access requires MFA and a current device compliance check. Public Wi-Fi is permitted only when the VPN is established first.

## 2. Session safety
Remote sessions lock after ten minutes of inactivity. Split tunneling is disabled for Restricted services. Workers must not access Restricted records from shared kiosks or borrowed devices.

## 3. Third-party access
Vendor remote access requires a named sponsor, system owner approval, and a time-boxed account expiring within seven days. Vendor sessions are logged and reviewed by the sponsor.

## 4. Lost remote devices
Workers notify the Security Steward within one hour of discovering a lost remote device. The service desk revokes active remote sessions and initiates a remote wipe where supported.
