# Acceptable Use Policy
SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION POLICY
NOT AN ACTUAL ORGANIZATION POLICY

## 1. Authorized work
Cooperative devices and accounts are for authorized work. Limited personal browsing is permitted if it does not interfere with duties or introduce security risk. Users must not share managed devices with family members.

## 2. Software and information sharing
Employees install software only from the approved catalog. Sending Restricted information through personal email or public file-sharing accounts is prohibited. Public material may be shared after its owner confirms the classification.

## 3. Reporting and monitoring
Users report suspected phishing through the report button promptly. Security logs from cooperative services are reviewed for protection and troubleshooting under the fictional monitoring notice; they are not a substitute for incident reports.

## 4. Use of automation
Automated tools may process Internal data only in approved local workspaces. Restricted content may not be pasted into external generative services without written Security Steward authorization.
